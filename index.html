I build AI systems that do real work. I started as a software developer, moved into data science, and today I design multi-agent architectures, tool contracts over MCP and LLM workflows that run in production — the unglamorous part where a model has to be grounded, observable and cheap enough to survive a Monday.

At Softtek's AI Center of Excellence I lead the agentic track: the reference agent architecture reused across client engagements, MCP servers that expose enterprise systems as governed tools, and pipelines that turn unstructured documents into ontologies and decision models so agents reason over business rules instead of raw text. Outside of that I build small games, mostly to keep my hands on the part of software where you can feel the result right away.

Autism and neurodivergence, in plain terms.

Why this exists

I'm autistic. Not as a warning label, and not as a condition I'm fighting: it's how I process information, focus and communicate. I don't experience it as a deficit, and I'm not going to pretend it is one.

This page is the list I wish I'd had when I started reading about it: material that treats neurodivergence as part of a person instead of an error to correct. I'm not a clinician and nobody paid me to write this. It's just what helped me.

    The org behind "nothing about us without us". Their resources page is the best single starting point.

  • Reframing Autism

Language

Words carry assumptions. This is what I use, and why:

autistic person
Identity-first is the community default. "Person with autism" is usually what institutions say; it isn't a crime, but it's a tell.
suffers from autism
No. Autistic people are not suffering by definition. Some things are hard; the autism itself isn't the wound.
high / low functioning
Describes how convenient you are to the people around you, not what you need. "Support needs" is the honest version.
special interest
A deep, sustained interest. Calling it an obsession says more about the observer than about the person.
masking
The work of hiding autistic traits to pass. It's expensive, and it's why a lot of people burn out before anyone notices.

What I don't link: organisations that treat autism as a disease to be eliminated, or that sell compliance-based therapy as the default. That's a values choice, not a claim of neutrality.

Lenguaje

Las palabras traen supuestos. Esto es lo que uso y por qué:

persona autista
Identidad primero: es lo que usa la comunidad. "Persona con autismo" suele ser lenguaje institucional; no es un crimen, pero delata de dónde viene.
sufre de autismo
No. No estamos sufriendo por definición. Hay cosas que cuestan; el autismo en sí no es la herida.
alto / bajo funcionamiento
Describe lo cómodo que eres para los demás, no lo que necesitas. "Necesidades de apoyo" es la versión honesta.
interés especial
Un interés profundo y sostenido. Llamarlo obsesión habla más de quien observa que de quien lo vive.
masking
El trabajo de esconder rasgos autistas para pasar desapercibido. Sale caro, y es la razón por la que mucha gente se quema antes de que nadie lo note.

Lo que no enlazo: organizaciones que tratan el autismo como una enfermedad a eliminar, o que venden terapias de obediencia como norma. Es una decisión de valores, no una pretensión de neutralidad.

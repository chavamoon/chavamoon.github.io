QUANTAAndroid · in testing

An endless momentum platformer. You never stop running: dash through hazards, chain combos, and race the same daily track as everyone else. Levels are seeded and the physics are deterministic, so the same seed is the same track for every player.

QUANTA is in closed testing on Google Play. There is no public download link yet. More experiments are on the way — the small ones end up here first.

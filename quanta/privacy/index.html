Privacy Policy — QUANTA

Last updated: October 14, 2026

QUANTA is an Android game developed by Salvador Luna ("chavamoon") — "we", "us". This policy explains what data the game processes, why, with whom it is shared, and what you can do about it. It applies to the app published on Google Play as com.quanta.runner.

In one sentence: QUANTA has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone, contacts, photos or location. It shows ads through Google AdMob (which uses the advertising ID) and, only if you choose to take part, it posts a nickname you pick and your score to a world leaderboard.

1. Data the game processes

a) Advertising ID and ad data (Google AdMob)

The game shows interstitial ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). That SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • the Android advertising ID;
  • the device IP address (and the city-level approximate location derived from it);
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language);
  • data about ad interactions (impressions, clicks, errors).

Purpose: to serve and measure ads. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy, policies.google.com/technologies/ads). On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.

Where required (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), the game shows a Google User Messaging Platform consent form before initializing the SDK. Without consent, ads are non-personalized.

b) World leaderboard (optional)

The game has a daily leaderboard powered by CheddaBoards (api.cheddaboards.com), a third-party service. If you take part, the following is sent to that service:

  • an anonymous identifier generated on your device — no name, email or account, just a random code;
  • the nickname you choose (3–16 characters: letters, digits or _), or the one the server assigns if you skip it (for example Player_1234);
  • your score, your best streak and technical run data (a play-session identifier).

The nickname and the score are public inside the game — they appear in the world Top. The anonymous identifier is never shown to other players.

Taking part is optional and reversible: the leaderboard screen has a "Join the world leaderboard" checkbox. Turn it off and the game stops sending data. To have data already on the server deleted, email us (see §8).

c) Data stored only on your phone

The game stores a configuration file in the app's private storage with your records, preferences, progress and the anonymous leaderboard identifier. That file is not sent anywhere, beyond the data described in (a) and (b), and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

d) What the game does NOT do

  • No sign-up and no login.
  • No real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or files.
  • No precise (GPS) location.
  • No microphone and no camera.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No profiling of our own: the leaderboard only sorts by score.

2. Why the data is used

  • Advertising: serving and measuring the ads that fund the game (advertising ID and Google SDK data).
  • App functionality: running the world leaderboard and the rival ghost (anonymous identifier, nickname, score).
  • Local operation: remembering records and preferences on your device.

Legal bases where applicable (GDPR): consent for personalized ads, and performance of the service you asked for for the leaderboard when you turn it on.

3. Children

QUANTA is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

4. Who it is shared with

  • Google (Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK): advertising ID, IP address, device data and ad interactions. Google's privacy policy.
  • CheddaBoards (api.cheddaboards.com): anonymous identifier, nickname, score and streak — the service that hosts the leaderboard.

Nobody else, unless we are legally required to.

5. Security

All communication with those services uses HTTPS. The game handles no sensitive information and no credentials. No system is 100% infallible, but by not processing identifiable personal data the risk stays minimal.

6. Retention

Local data lives on your phone until you uninstall the app, or clear it from Android settings. Leaderboard data (nickname, score) stays on CheddaBoards' server while you take part, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Turn the leaderboard off inside the game (leaderboard screen → "Join the world leaderboard").
  • Reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Request access, correction, deletion or objection for leaderboard data: email us (see §8). In the EEA, the UK or Switzerland you have rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR.
  • Uninstall the app to erase all local data.

QUANTA

Endless momentum platformer · Android

Coming soon. QUANTA is in closed testing on Google Play right now. There is no public download link yet.

QUANTA is a run game with no run button. You move forward on your own and keep accelerating, so the whole game is about reading what comes next: jump, double jump, wall jump, and dash.

The run

  • Dash is the only defence. It turns you into a ghost for a fraction of a second so you pass through a hazard instead of breaking it. Hazards stay where they are, so the level never gets easier. It costs a cooldown, and you can see that cost in the dash bar.
  • One hit ends the run. No lives, no shield, no health bar.
  • The combo is the score. Pop balloons in chains to climb the ranks, from D up to SSS. Let the window expire and you drop a single rank, so chasing one more cluster is always a real decision.
  • A daily run. Every day has its own track, built from a seed everyone shares. Play it, share the seed, then race your own best run as a ghost with a live distance delta.
  • A world leaderboard for the daily run — optional, with a nickname you choose.

Details

  • Endless procedural levels that get faster and tighter with distance.
  • Hand-tuned chunks on top of the procedural generator, so the hard parts are designed, not random.
  • Desktop-style controls on a phone: tap anywhere to jump, swipe right to dash.
  • A curated bank of real science facts, shown when you die and on the title screen.
  • All art and music generated in code — small download, no asset packs.
  • English and Spanish, following your device language.

Tech

Built with Godot 4.6, targeting Android. Levels are generated from a seed and the physics are deterministic, so the same seed is the same track for everyone.
